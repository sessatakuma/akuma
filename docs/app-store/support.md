# AkuMa iOS support

For a reproducible bug, open an issue at
https://github.com/sessatakuma/akuma/issues. Include your app version/build, iPhone
or iPad model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Use a short non-sensitive
Japanese example; do not post private text or personal information in a public issue.

## Analysis is unavailable

Check your internet connection and retry. The app keeps your draft and last
completed result. You can open the saved result or return to editing your text.

## A reading or accent looks wrong

Tap the word, edit its kana reading or select a pitch-drop position, preview the
result, and tap Done. Undo and redo are available in the result options menu.

## Share a result

Use Share on the result screen and choose a destination in the system share sheet.

## Privacy requests

The publisher must supply a private privacy-contact channel before App Store
submission. Do not send personal or sensitive data through public GitHub issues.
